{# Sign-in-with-passkey modal — shown on the empty-state /me page so a user who lost their cookie can recover the session their passkey is anchored to. Trigger: any element with data-modal-trigger="restore-session". Included by /me/me_first_run.html. #}

Sign in with a passkey

If you added a passkey on this device before, your device will prompt you to unlock with fingerprint, face, PIN, or security key.

Signing in overwrites this browser's current session. Any data only saved here will be hidden.
