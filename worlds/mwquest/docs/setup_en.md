# MWQuest Randomizer Setup Guide

## Required Software

- [MultiworldGG](github.com/MultiworldGG/MultiworldGG/releases/latest)
- [The MWQuest apworld](https://github.com/NewSoupVi/Archipelago/releases), 
  if not bundled with your version of MultiworldGG / AP

## Optional Software

- [MWQuest AP Tracker](https://github.com/palex00/ap-quest-tracker/releases/latest), for use with
[PopTracker](https://github.com/black-sliver/PopTracker/releases)

## How to play

First, you need a room to connect to. For this, you or someone you know has to generate a game.  
This will not be explained here,
but you can check the [MultiworldGG Setup Guide](/tutorial/Archipelago/setup_en#generating-a-game).

You also need to have [MultiworldGG](github.com/MultiworldGG/MultiworldGG/releases/latest) installed
and the [The MWQuest apworld](https://github.com/NewSoupVi/Archipelago/releases) installed into MultiworldGG.

From here, connecting to your MWQuest slot is easy. There are two scenarios.

### Webhost Room

If your room is hosted on a WebHost (e.g. [multiworld.gg](multiworld.gg)),
you should be able to simply click on your name in the player list.  
This will open the MultiworldGG Launcher
and ask you whether you want to connect with the Text Client or the MWQuest Client.  
Choose "MWQuest Client". The rest should happen completely automatically and you should be able to play MWQuest.

### Locally hosted room

If your room does not have a WebHost room page available, you can launch MWQuest manually.  

Open the MultiworldGG Launcher, and then select the MWQuest Client from the list.  
After a short while, the MWQuest client should open.  
Enter the server address at the top and click "Connect".  
Then, enter your name. If a password exists, enter the password.  
You should now be connected and able to play MWQuest.

## Switching Rooms

The MWQuest Client can seamlessly switch rooms without restarting.

Simply click the "Disconnect" button, then connect to a different slot/room.

## Auto-Tracking

AP Quest has a fully functional map tracker that supports auto-tracking.

1. Download [MWQuest AP Tracker](https://github.com/palex00/ap-quest-tracker/releases/latest) and
[PopTracker](https://github.com/black-sliver/PopTracker/releases).
2. Put the tracker pack into packs/ in your PopTracker install.
3. Open PopTracker, and load the MWQuest pack.
4. For autotracking, click on the "AP" symbol at the top.
5. Enter the Archipelago server address (the one you connected your client to), slot name, and password.
